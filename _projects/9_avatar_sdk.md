## Avatar SDK

At [itSeez3D](https://itseez3d.com/) I worked on a very interesting project called [Avatar SDK](https://avatarsdk.com).
Avatar SDK is a deep-learning based pipeline for human digitization.

## Mobile 3D scanner (2013–2017)

With itSeez, and later itSeez3D I participated in the development of the [3D scanning software](https://itseez3d.com) for various structured light sensors.
Our results are close to those of professional 3D scanners, for 10-100x less money!
